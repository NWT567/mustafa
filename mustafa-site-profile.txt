 BRAND INTRODUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Brand name:
 Mustafa Coffee House

Brand positioning:
 Premium coffee house, bakery, and dessert experience with a warm
 espresso-and-gold identity, elevated service, and a luxury cafe mood.

Core promise:
 Slow-roasted beans, handcrafted drinks, fresh bakery items, and a
 refined place for dine-in, order-ahead, reservations, and rewards.

Primary site sections:
 1. Home
 2. Menu / Order
 3. Reserve
 4. About
 5. Loyalty
 6. Franchise
 7. Careers
 8. Login / Sign in

----------------------------------------------------------------
 MISSION & GOAL
----------------------------------------------------------------
Mission:
 To make coffee feel ceremonial, generous, and memorable through
 premium ingredients, thoughtful service, and a warm brand experience.

Goal:
 To build a recognizable cafe brand that supports order-ahead, table
 reservations, loyalty rewards, hiring, and franchise growth while
 keeping the same refined visual language across the site.

Brand values:
 - Craft
 - Hospitality
 - Consistency
 - Warmth
 - Premium presentation
 - Modern convenience

----------------------------------------------------------------
 PRIMARY COLOR PALETTE
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Primary palette direction:
 Warm light theme with espresso, cream, caramel, and gold accents.

 Core colors:
 - Background: warm off-white / cream
 - Foreground: deep espresso brown
 - Primary accent: gold
 - Secondary accent: caramel
 - Supporting neutrals: muted beige, soft border gray

 Usage:
 - Backgrounds and page surfaces stay light
 - Espresso is used for readable text
 - Gold is used for highlights, borders, button fills, and icons
 - Caramel is used for gradients and warm emphasis

----------------------------------------------------------------
 SECONDARY COLOR PALETTE
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Secondary tones:
 - Soft latte
 - Warm muted gold
 - Sand
 - Mocha
 - Smoke / overlay beige

 Usage:
 - Secondary cards
 - Hover states
 - Subtle borders
 - Decorative gradients
 - Badge backgrounds

----------------------------------------------------------------
 TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Typography direction:
 Editorial, premium, and high-contrast.

 Headline style:
 - Large serif headlines
 - Strong weight contrast
 - Tight line-height
 - Used for hero sections, section titles, and feature cards

 Body style:
 - Clean sans-serif for labels, helper text, inputs, and navigation
 - Small uppercase tracking for eyebrow labels and utility text

 Common text treatments:
 - Serif display for emotional and premium moments
 - Sans-serif for clarity and usability
 - Gold gradient only for decorative emphasis
 - Espresso text on light backgrounds for readability

----------------------------------------------------------------
 PRIMARY & ALTERNATIVE FONTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Primary display font:
 - DM Serif Display

 Primary body font:
 - Fira Sans

 Alternative / fallback stack:
 - Display: ui-serif, Georgia, serif
 - Body: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif

 Use cases:
 - DM Serif Display for headings, hero copy, card titles, and brand
   statements
 - Fira Sans for navigation, buttons, inputs, helper text, and forms

----------------------------------------------------------------
 LOGO CONCEPTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Main logo idea:
 A refined Mustafa mark paired with a wordmark that feels premium,
 warm, and hospitality-led.

Concept directions:
 - Compact icon mark for small placements
 - Wordmark for hero headers and sign-in areas
 - Light-background variant for the current site theme
 - High-contrast usage for clean reading on cream backgrounds

Brand feel:
 - Premium cafe
 - Artisan coffee
 - Modern hospitality
 - Elegant and memorable

----------------------------------------------------------------
 LOGO APPLICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Where the logo appears:
 - Navigation bar
 - Home hero
 - Login page
 - Footer
 - Brand deck / PDF cover
 - Social or promotional assets

 Recommended usage:
 - Use the wordmark on larger surfaces
 - Use the compact mark where space is limited
 - Keep the logo clear against cream or light surfaces
 - Avoid placing the logo over busy imagery without contrast support

----------------------------------------------------------------
 LOGO CLEAR SPACE RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Clear space rule:
 Keep enough breathing room around the logo so no text, borders, or
 imagery crowd it.

 Practical rule:
 - Maintain at least one logo-mark height of clear space on all sides
 - Do not overlay other labels directly on the logo
 - Do not squeeze the logo into tight image corners

----------------------------------------------------------------
 LOGO MINIMUM SIZE
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Minimum size guidance:
 - Compact icon: keep large enough to remain recognizable in navigation
 - Wordmark: keep large enough to read clearly on desktop and mobile

 Practical rule:
 - Do not shrink the logo until the mark loses detail or the wordmark
   becomes hard to read
 - Use the compact mark for tiny placements and the wordmark for
   headers, footers, and sign-in screens

----------------------------------------------------------------
 INCORRECT LOGO USAGE
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Do not:
 - Stretch the logo horizontally or vertically
 - Add random glow effects that weaken contrast
 - Place the logo on a busy image without a contrast wash
 - Change the logo colors arbitrarily
 - Crop the logo too tightly
 - Rotate or skew the logo
 - Use a dark-only asset on a light background if contrast suffers

----------------------------------------------------------------
 ICONOGRAPHY GUIDELINES
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Style:
 - Simple, clean, and line-based icons
 - Consistent stroke weight
 - Minimal detail
 - Warm metallic/gold accents for emphasis

 Usage:
 - Navigation utilities
 - CTA buttons
 - Section badges
 - Form fields
 - Rewards and loyalty visuals

 Rules:
 - Keep icons readable at small sizes
 - Use one visual style across the site
 - Do not mix overly detailed and ultra-minimal icons in the same UI

----------------------------------------------------------------
 LOGO VARIATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Recommended variations:
 - Full wordmark
 - Compact mark
 - Light-background version
 - Icon-only version

 Suggested usage:
 - Wordmark: headers, login, footer
 - Compact mark: favicon, mobile header, small badges
 - Light-background version: cream and white surfaces
 - Icon-only: tight spaces and social-style placements
